How to Play

Top Bar
●● N online — tap to see who's in the world
👤Your name — tap for your character card, quests, and skills
⚡Energy — used for movement and physical skills. Regens over time.
🔮Mana — used for magic skills like Dazzle. Regens over time.
❤️HP — your health. Reach 0 and you respawn at the start.
The Room
🟡Gold words — tap them to discover objects and open actions
🔵Discovered chips — tap to open actions again. Show your progress.
📍Discovered N/N · Events N/N — how much of the room you've explored
Movement
↑Direction buttons — bright = available, dim = no exit that way
Bottom Bar
🎒Bag — opens your inventory. Swipe to see more items.
✋Hand pills — shows what you're holding. Tap to open actions. Dims and undims to show attack speed in combat.
✨Skill pill — appears in combat next to your weapon. Gold glow = ready to use. Tap to activate.
⚔️Retreat — appears in combat. Staged escape: melee → ranged → notice. 90% chance each step.
Combat
👁️Notice — creature spotted you. Not in combat yet — you can still move freely.
🏃Ranged — combat starts. Bag locks. Creature is closing in.
⚔️Melee — your weapons auto-attack. Watch the hand pills fill up.
💬Combat panel — splits enemy hits (left) from your hits (right) so you can track both.
Items & Crafting
🤲Combine — hold two items and tap one — the combine button appears if a recipe exists.
🔍Look — look at a held item to see its stats, XP, and available skills.
